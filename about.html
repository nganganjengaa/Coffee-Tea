About Us

Aroma Coffee & Tea House, your premier destination for premium coffee and tea experiences. Was established in 1950 by Muhehu Mundu Aroma to roast, package, and supply high-quality coffee and tea sourced from the volcanic regions of East Africa, serving as the regions' first commercial coffee and tea supplier.

Our Mission

To exponentially advance our mission to improve communities with every cup of exceptional coffee and tea.

Our Vision

To be recognized as Africa's leading coffee and tea brand, known for our exceptional quality and commitment to sustainability.
